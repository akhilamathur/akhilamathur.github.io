Alive Intelligence: Make for India

Localized product innovation for the Indian market — consumer insight translated into a named, shipped feature set across the Galaxy A and M series.

The problem

Global smartphone features are rarely built with India's specific mix of languages, connectivity, and user habits in mind — they're localized after the fact, usually just in translation. Samsung's India Product Planning team took a different approach: build India-first, informed by consumer research grounded in how people in India actually use their phones day to day, and let those features travel outward from there.

The work

As part of the Product Innovation team leading localized product strategy for India, I worked on consumer insight that fed directly into a named feature set Samsung branded "Alive Intelligence" — shipped first on the Galaxy A51 in February 2020, aimed squarely at the fast-moving, multilingual habits of Gen Z users in India.

What shipped

2020

Alive Intelligence launches on Galaxy A51

Samsung's own newsroom described it as "cool and intelligent 'Make for India' innovations... based on extensive consumer research in India," shipping four features: Useful Cards, Multilingual Typing, Finder, and Smart Crop — each designed around what the research surfaced about how Indian Gen Z users actually manage their phones.

2020–2022

Extended across the A and M series

The same research-led approach to India-first features continued informing the affordable and mid-range Galaxy lineups that make up the bulk of Samsung's volume in the Indian market during this period.

The throughline

Localization is often treated as a translation checklist. This was the opposite: research-led features designed for how a specific market actually behaves, then shipped with their own name and identity rather than folded quietly into a global feature list. It's the same instinct that shaped the Infinity U naming work — that framing and cultural specificity are themselves part of the design, not an afterthought to it.
